Privacy Policy

IMFine — last updated 11 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects personal data when you use the IMFine check-in application (the "Service"). We aim to comply with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who we are

Data controller: IMFine. Contact: imfine-admin@ymailzone.com.

What we collect

  • Account information: your name and email address.
  • Check-in settings: your daily check-in time and time zone.
  • Guardian information: the names and email addresses you add for the people you nominate to be alerted.
  • Activity records: your daily check-ins and system events (for example, a missed check-in and the alerts that followed).
  • Location: we do not track your location continuously. Location may be captured only at the moment a check-in is missed, so that it can be shared with your Guardians to help locate you.
  • Payment information: subscription payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your card details; we retain only a subscription reference and status.

How we use it

  • To operate the daily check-in and, if a check-in is missed, to notify your nominated Guardians.
  • To send you and your Guardians notifications by email and (where enabled) push notification.
  • To manage subscriptions and provide customer support.

Our legal bases (GDPR) include performance of a contract with you and your consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

Who we share it with

Your check-in status and, if applicable, last known location are shared with the Guardians you nominate. We do not sell your personal data.

Where data is stored

Data is stored in our database hosted in the Singapore region. Some processors may transfer data internationally under appropriate safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. You may delete your account and associated data at any time by contacting us. Some records may be retained where required by law.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, and to withdraw consent or object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email above.

Security

We use access controls and database-level security rules so that each user can access only their own data, and Guardians can see only the person they are linked to. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

The Service is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the updated version here with a new date.
